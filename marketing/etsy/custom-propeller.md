**Description (paste as plain text; Etsy does not render formatting)**

Have your own propeller design? I'll laser-cut it from 1/32" balsa so every blade is identical and ready to form. Perfect for Science Olympiad Flight teams testing blade shapes, or anyone who has outgrown off-the-shelf props.

HOW IT WORKS
1. Choose how many sets you want (2 to 6; each set = 2 blades = 1 propeller). Multiple different designs are fine – the count is total sets.
2. At checkout, describe your design in the personalization box.
3. After you order, send the design file in an Etsy message. DXF, SVG, or PDF is ideal; any clear image works if you also tell me the overall blade length.
4. I cut, pack, and ship within 3–5 business days of receiving your file.

WHAT'S INCLUDED (per order)
• Laser-cut 1/32" balsa blades for every set ordered
• Adjustable-pitch propeller hubs
• A 4 x 2 inch cardboard tube for wet-forming the blades
• Everything else needed to complete the propellers

WHAT YOU WILL NEED (not included)
• Super glue (CA)
• Water for wet-forming

DESIGN GUIDELINES
• Blade length up to about 12 inches (300 mm) per blade – message me first if yours is longer
• Keep the blade root wide enough to glue to the hub
• No design? Try our Propeller Kit listing for our own long-flight blade shape

PRICING
2 sets $12.99, then $4 per additional set – pick the quantity in the drop-down.

SHIPPING & RETURNS
Ships from the US via USPS. US addresses only. Because each order is cut to your design, custom propellers cannot be returned. If anything arrives damaged, message me within 7 days with a photo and I'll recut it.
