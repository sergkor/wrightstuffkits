**Description (paste as plain text; Etsy does not render formatting)**

Build two competition-grade balsa propellers for your Science Olympiad Flight airplane. The blade shape is our own design optimized for long indoor flights, cut from 1/32" balsa and formed on the included jig. The adjustable-pitch hub lets you change blade angle to match your rubber motor and ceiling height – the single biggest tuning lever in indoor free flight.

WHAT'S IN THE KIT
• 1/32" balsa blade blanks for 2 propellers
• Forming jig
• Adjustable-pitch propeller hub

WHAT YOU WILL NEED (not included)
• Super glue (CA)
• Hobby knife
• Water for wet-forming the blades (the prop-building steps are in our airplane kit instructions; message me if you need them on their own)

GOOD TO KNOW
• Same propeller design that ships in our Intermediate and Advanced Kits – buy this as spares, or to upgrade a plane that came with a plastic prop
• Blades are shipped flat; you form them on the jig
• Fits standard 1/32" (0.8 mm) prop shaft wire; message me if your plane uses a different shaft
