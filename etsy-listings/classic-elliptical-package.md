Two designs, one box. This package gives you the materials to build ONE Elliptical plane and ONE Classic plane – our two competition airframes for Science Olympiad Division C Flight (2027 rules) – so your team can fly both, compare them head-to-head, and pick the design that wins in your gym. • Carbon fiber rods for both motor sticks and tail booms
• Lightweight Mylar covering for both planes

HOW TO BUILD IT
1. Sort the laser-cut sheets by design – the Classic and Elliptical parts are labeled separately in the instructions.
2. Build the Classic first if you're newer to Mylar: straight edges are easier to cover.
3. Fit the carbon fiber motor sticks and tail booms, then cover both planes with Mylar using spray adhesive.
4. Wet-form the balsa propeller blades on the jig, let them dry, and glue them to the adjustable hubs.
5. Make up rubber motors from the included 1/8" FAI rubber and O-rings.
6. Test-fly both with the PVC prop, then move to the balsa props and tune pitch using the trimming guide. Fly them back-to-back and keep notes.

WHO IT'S FOR
