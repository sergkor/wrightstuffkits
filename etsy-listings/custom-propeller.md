Have your own propeller design? I'll laser-cut it from 1/32" balsa so every blade is identical, light, and ready to form. Made for Science Olympiad Flight teams testing blade shapes – or anyone who has outgrown off-the-shelf props – and shipped with adjustable hubs and a forming tube so you can finish the propeller the day it arrives.

HOW IT WORKS
1. Choose how many sets you want (2 to 6). Each set = 2 blades = 1 propeller. Multiple different designs are fine – the count is the total number of sets.
2. Order, then send your design in an Etsy message. DXF is ideal; SVG or PDF also work. A clear photo or drawing works too if you tell me the overall blade length.
3. I cut, pack, and ship within 3–5 business days of receiving your file.

WHAT'S INCLUDED
• Laser-cut 1/32" balsa blades for every set ordered
• Adjustable-pitch propeller hubs (one per set) so you can tune blade angle to your motor and ceiling
• A 4 x 2 inch cardboard tube for wet-forming the blades
• Steel wire for prop shafts
• Everything else needed to complete the propellers – just add glue

WHAT YOU WILL NEED (not included)
• Super glue (CA)
• Water for wet-forming the blades

DESIGN GUIDELINES
• Blade material: 1/32" (0.8 mm) balsa
• Blade length up to about 12 inches (300 mm) per blade – message me first if yours is longer [TODO: confirm max cut size for your laser bed]
• Keep the blade root wide enough to glue securely into the hub [TODO: state the hub slot width]
• Send one closed outline per blade; I'll mirror or duplicate as needed
• No design of your own? Our Propeller Kit listing has our own long-flight blade shape

SHIPPING & RETURNS
Ships from the US via USPS (Ground Advantage or Priority). US addresses only. Because every order is cut to your design, custom propellers cannot be returned. If anything arrives damaged, message me within 7 days with a photo and I'll recut it.

Not sure if your file will work? Message me before ordering and I'll take a look.
