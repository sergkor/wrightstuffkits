Built for maximum flight time. The Elliptical Kit is our top-performing rubber-powered indoor free-flight airplane for Science Olympiad Division C Flight (2027 rules), designed for 3+ minute flights, with enough parts to build TWO complete planes. The elliptical wing and endplate stabilizer squeeze every second out of the rubber motor, and the adjustable-pitch balsa propellers let you tune the plane to your ceiling height.

WHAT'S INCLUDED
• Laser-cut balsa and plywood airframe parts for 2 airplanes – no tracing, no cutting from templates
• Carbon fiber rods for a stiff, light motor stick and tail boom
• Lightweight Mylar covering for the wing and stabilizer
• Materials, jig, and 1/32" balsa blanks to build 2 balsa wood propellers
• 2 adjustable-pitch propeller hubs so you can change blade angle to match your motor and ceiling
• Plus one ready-to-use 24 cm PVC propeller to fly on day one
• 1/8" FAI rubber for motors, plus O-rings
• Steel wire for prop shafts and hooks
• Step-by-step instructions: assembly, propeller building, rubber motor making, winding tips, and trimming

WHAT YOU WILL NEED (not included)
• Super glue (CA)
• Hobby knife
• Spray adhesive (for covering)
• Pliers
• A rubber winder

SPECIFICATIONS
• Designed to Science Olympiad Division C 2027 Flight rules (always confirm against the current rules manual and your event supervisor)
• Design target: 3+ minute flights in a full-height gym
• Builds 2 planes per kit
• Wing: elliptical planform, Mylar covered; stabilizer with endplates
• Propellers: 2 buildable balsa props with adjustable-pitch hubs + 1 ready-made 24 cm PVC prop
• Skill level: intermediate to advanced – covering a curved outline with Mylar takes patience

HOW TO BUILD IT
1. Pop the laser-cut parts from the sheets and assemble the elliptical wing outline, stabilizer, and endplates following the numbered steps.
2. Fit the carbon fiber motor stick and tail boom.
3. Cover the flying surfaces with Mylar using spray adhesive.
4. Wet-form the balsa propeller blades on the jig, let them dry, and glue them to the adjustable hubs.
5. Make up a rubber motor from the included 1/8" FAI rubber and O-rings.
6. Start with the PVC prop for test flights, then switch to your balsa props and tune pitch and motor using the trimming guide.
Build the second plane as your backup or to test a different setup.

WHO IT'S FOR
