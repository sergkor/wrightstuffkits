Build two competition-grade balsa propellers for your Science Olympiad Flight airplane. The blade outline is our own design, optimized for long indoor flights, laser-cut from 1/32" balsa and wet-formed on the included jig. The adjustable-pitch hub lets you change blade angle to match your rubber motor and ceiling height – the single biggest tuning lever in indoor free flight.

HOW TO BUILD IT
1. Soak the balsa blades in warm water for a few minutes until flexible.
2. Wrap them onto the forming jig, secure, and let them dry fully (overnight is ideal).
3. Glue the dried blades into the adjustable hub at a starting pitch, keeping both blades matched.
4. Fit the prop shaft, balance the propeller, and mount it to your motor stick.
5. Test fly and adjust pitch: more pitch for a low ceiling and slow climb, less pitch for a tall gym.
Detailed prop-building steps are included in our airplane kit instructions – message me if you'd like them on their own.

WHO IT'S FOR
Flight competitors who want a lighter, more efficient prop than a stock PVC one, coaches who need spares for the whole team, and anyone experimenting with pitch. Have your own blade design? See our Custom Laser-cut Propeller listing.
