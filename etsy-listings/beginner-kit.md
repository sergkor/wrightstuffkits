New to Science Olympiad Flight? Start here. The Beginner Kit is a forgiving, rubber-powered indoor free-flight airplane designed to the Division C 2027 Flight rules, with enough laser-cut parts to build TWO complete planes. Tissue covering and plywood-reinforced parts make it tough enough to survive the learning curve, and the step-by-step instructions take you from the first glue joint to your first trimmed flight.

WHAT'S INCLUDED
• Laser-cut balsa and plywood airframe parts for 2 airplanes – no tracing, no cutting from templates
• Tissue covering for wings and stabilizers
• One ready-to-use 24 cm PVC propeller (swap it between your two planes)
• 1/8" FAI rubber for motors, plus O-rings
• Steel wire for prop shafts and hooks
• Step-by-step instructions: assembly, rubber motor making, winding tips, and trimming

WHAT YOU WILL NEED (not included)
• Super glue (CA)
• Hobby knife
• Spray adhesive (for covering)
• Pliers
• A rubber winder

SPECIFICATIONS
• Designed to Science Olympiad Division C 2027 Flight rules (always confirm against the current rules manual and your event supervisor)
• Finished flying weight approx. 9.5 g – slightly heavier than our Mylar kits, by design, for durability
• Builds 2 planes per kit
• Propeller: 24 cm PVC, ready to fly
• Skill level: beginner – first build takes an evening or two
• Recommended age 14+ (small parts, hobby knife required)

HOW TO BUILD IT
1. Pop the laser-cut parts from the sheets and glue the motor stick, wing, and tail frames following the numbered steps.
2. Cover the wing and stabilizer with the included tissue using spray adhesive.
3. Make up a rubber motor from the included 1/8" FAI rubber and O-rings.
4. Mount the 24 cm PVC propeller, balance, and follow the trimming guide for your first test glides and powered flights.
Build the second plane as your backup or to try a different trim.

WHO IT'S FOR
First-year Flight competitors, coaches outfitting a new team, and anyone who wants a reliable indoor rubber plane without the fuss of Mylar. Ready for a more competitive design? See our Classic Kit and Elliptical Kit listings.
